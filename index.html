About me

Your career goals. My support.

Deborah Nwoye helps African professionals abroad turn their experience into strong CVs, apply to targeted job ads and build outstanding LinkedIn profiles.

Every piece of work is shaped by real experience and hard-won skills. I believe in transforming profiles and experience into opportunities.

Deborah works with

  • Nigerians and other African professionals already living in the UK, Canada and the US.
  • Professionals seeking better career opportunities who need help identifying suitable roles, positioning their experience and navigating applications.
  • Experienced professionals abroad who want to change careers or move into more suitable roles.
